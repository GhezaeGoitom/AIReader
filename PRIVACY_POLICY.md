# Privacy Policy for AI Reader

AI Reader respects your privacy.

## Information Collection and Use

AI Reader does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

The app may process user-selected PDF files locally on the device in order to provide reading functionality. Text extracted from PDFs is used only for app features and is not stored permanently.

## Third-Party Services

AI Reader may interact with third-party APIs to provide certain features (such as AI-based analysis or music playback). These services are used only to deliver app functionality and do not receive personal user information.

## Data Storage

All processing happens locally on the device unless explicitly required for a feature. No personal data is saved on external servers by the app.

## Children’s Privacy

AI Reader does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future as the app evolves.
